                environmental and endogenous rhythms and their interplay on electrophysiological dynamics of neuronal populations. Therefore,

                suprachiasmatic nucleus and the nucleus accumbens under regular light conditions and constant darkness. Using reﬁned
                descriptive and statistical analyses, we systematically proﬁled LFP time series activity. We show that both environmental and
                endogenous rhythms strongly inuence the rhythmicity of LFP signals and their frequency components, but also shape neuronal
